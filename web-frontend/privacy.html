Your data, in plain language.

LooksGood helps users create, share, and manage fashion content. This page explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how to request help or deletion.

1. Information we collect

We may collect account details such as email address, username, password hash, profile bio, and profile photo. When you create content, we may also process images, videos, captions, comments, chat messages, stories, AI prompts, and commerce-related product tags.

2. Device permissions

The Android app may request access to your camera, microphone, and media library so you can capture or upload photos, videos, audio, profile media, reels, and stories. These permissions are only used to support features you choose to use.

3. How we use data

We use your data to authenticate your account, show your profile and feed, deliver social features, process uploads, generate AI content, manage subscriptions, and improve product quality, safety, and reliability.

4. Sharing

Content you post or share inside LooksGood can be visible to other users based on your account and privacy settings. We may also use service providers for hosting, analytics, authentication, media delivery, AI processing, and payments needed to run the service.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep data for as long as it is needed to operate the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies. If you want us to delete your account data, contact us through the support channel listed below.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect account and content data. No internet-based system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, so please use strong passwords and protect access to your devices.

7. Contact

For privacy, data access, or deletion requests, contact the LooksGood team using the support details provided in the app listing or your customer support channel.

8. Updates

We may update this policy when the product changes. Material changes should be reflected on this page with an updated publication date.
